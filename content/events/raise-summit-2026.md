## GWTL @ RAISE Summit 2026

Global Women TechLeaders is proud to partner with RAISE Summit 2026, taking place July 8–9, 2026 at Le Carrousel du Louvre in Paris.

RAISE Summit brings together global AI leaders, C-suite executives, startups, investors, and policymakers for two days focused on AI leadership, enterprise adoption, investment, and the real-world impact of artificial intelligence.

For GWTL, this partnership matters because AI leadership is not just a technology conversation. It is a capital conversation, a governance conversation, and a decision-making conversation. Women must be visible, connected, and influential in the rooms where the next era of AI is being shaped.

### About RAISE Summit

RAISE Summit is positioned as a global stage for AI leadership, enterprise adoption, and ROI. The 2026 edition is expected to bring together:

- **9,000+** AI leaders and attendees
- **2,000+** companies
- **350+** speakers
- C-suite executives, founders, investors, policymakers, builders, and AI operators

The programme spans keynotes, panels, curated off-stage moments, startup activity, and RAISE Week side events across Paris.

### What We're Doing at RAISE Summit

- Representing GWTL's mission to accelerate women into technology leadership
- Creating visibility for women leaders in AI and technology
- Connecting our community to decision-makers, investors, founders, and enterprise AI leaders
- Strengthening GWTL's European AI leadership network
- Supporting access to one of Europe's major AI gatherings

### Who Should Attend

- Women tech leaders working in AI, data, product, engineering, governance, or digital transformation
- Founders and startup leaders building AI-native companies
- Corporate partners investing in inclusive AI leadership
- Investors and ecosystem builders looking for high-potential women-led ventures and leadership pipelines
- GWTL members ready to build visibility and relationships in the AI economy

## Event Details

- **Dates:** July 8–9, 2026
